AMSTERDAM, The Netherlands -- On December 14, 2025, the Amsterdam Mustangs secured a decisive 9-5 victory over the Amsterdam Snipers in a season opener rematch. In a high-offense affair, the Mustangs unleashed a dominant second-period performance to secure the victory and set a commanding tone for the season ahead.

The game's pivotal moment was a dominant second-period offensive surge by the Mustangs, where they scored five unanswered goals to break a 2-2 tie.

In a high-scoring contest featuring 14 total goals, key individual contributions were essential to driving the action. While several players from both teams had notable performances, the Mustangs' offense was fueled by a handful of exceptionally productive players who consistently created scoring opportunities.

Key offensive contributions for the Mustangs came from Jared Penner, who recorded a hat-trick and 2 assists, and Miska Braun, who had 2 goals and 3 assists. For the Snipers, Eric Kuiper and Fedor Tieleman each scored two goals. A notable event involved Mustangs forward Jeremie Gould who scored with a hard wrist shot that hit the inner top padding of the net and bounced out quickly. The play was not initially ruled a goal by the referees but was later confirmed by video review and is included in the final 9-5 score.

The Mustangs' special teams were a critical factor, operating at 50% on the power play and maintaining a perfect 100% penalty kill, while the Snipers failed to convert on any power-play opportunities. The Mustangs demonstrated superior special teams play, which contributed significantly to the outcome. Penalties were pretty even although highlighted by offsetting 10-minute misconducts in the third period.

After an evenly matched first period that promised a close game, the Amsterdam Mustangs proved they were the superior team. Their overwhelming five-goal offensive explosion in the second period created a lead that the Snipers could not overcome. While the Snipers mounted a spirited comeback attempt in the third, it was the Mustangs' defensive discipline and flawless penalty killing that ultimately secured the victory, weathering the storm before their own power play delivered the final blow. It was a statement victory to open the season, showcasing the Mustangs' offensive depth and special teams prowess in a decisive 9-5 win.
