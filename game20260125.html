AMSTERDAM, The Netherlands -- The Amsterdam Mustangs and Amsterdam Tigers 6 battled to a 4-4 tie in a heated rematch on January 25th, 2026. The contest was a back-and-forth affair that saw momentum swing wildly between the cross-town rivals. From a furious opening by the Tigers to a third-period comeback by the Mustangs, the outcome remained in balance until the final horn.

The Tigers stormed out of the gate, overwhelming the Mustangs with an offensive blitz that resulted in two goals within the first two minutes. Jesse Clarke opened the scoring just 41 seconds into the match. Less than two minutes later, Robin Rensen doubled the lead to put the Tigers firmly in control with an early 2-0 advantage.

After a difficult start, the Mustangs mounted a determined comeback in the second period, erasing the two-goal deficit. Chris Price got the Mustangs on the board with an unassisted goal at 10:11. Five minutes later, George Huber tied the game 2-2. However, the Tigers reclaimed the lead before the period ended when Kenneth McCann scored sending the home team into the final frame with a 3-2 lead.

The final period saw the Mustangs fight to comeback from behind. They capitalized on an early power play when Daniel Perrier scored. Then the Tigers responded with a powerplay goal of their own as Willem Jan van Loon found the back of the net off a feed from Travis Wetzlaugk to restore their one-goal lead. The game's frantic pace continued until Daniel Perrier scored his second of the period with an unassisted goal at 14:13 to tie the game 4-4 and secure the draw.

With the Mustangs having won the first game of their three-match series, this draw sets the stage for a decisive final game on March 7th to determine the season series winner.
