AMSTERDAM, The Netherlands -- The Amsterdam Mustangs secured a hard-fought 7-6 victory over rival Cool Mokum 2 on Sunday evening, a crucial win that clinched the season series between the two clubs. The high-scoring, back-and-forth contest saw multiple lead changes before the Mustangs ultimately held on for the win.

The game was defined by dramatic momentum swings, with each team establishing what appeared to be a commanding lead only to see the other mount a furious comeback. The contest was a testament to offensive resilience, keeping fans on the edge of their seats until the final buzzer.

Cool Mokum 2 stormed out of the gate, building a 2-0 lead in the first period on unassisted goals from Joel van Baarsen and Ole Hupkes. The Mustangs battled back to tie the game, beginning with a power-play goal from Chris Price, who snapped a wrist shot from the slot to get Amsterdam on the board. Shortly after, Miska Braun intercepted a puck at the blue line and fed Petteri Hyytiäinen, who deked out the goalie and scored with a backhand shot to make it 2-2. However, Cool Mokum retook the lead before the first intermission on a second goal from Hupkes and extended it to a formidable 5-2 advantage early in the second period with strikes from Zaran Zaitsoff and van Baarsen.

Facing a three-goal deficit, the Amsterdam Mustangs orchestrated the game's pivotal turning point. The team erupted for four unanswered goals in a dominant second-period display to seize control. The rally began with a goal from Krisztian Vizsy. The tying goal came when Jon Garrity's shot from the point was skillfully tip-passed by Jeremie Gould to Chris Price, who fired it home. The go-ahead goal came on the power play, as Nils Klok unleashed a slap shot from the point that Jeremie Gould masterfully tipped into the top corner of the net. Just 13 seconds later, Jon Garrity scored to cap the offensive burst, transforming a 5-2 deficit into a 6-5 lead for the Mustangs heading into the final frame.

The Mustangs extended their lead early in the third when Miska Braun found the back of the net to make it 7-5. Cool Mokum 2 refused to go quietly, capitalizing on a power-play opportunity with a goal from Zaran Zaitsoff to pull within one. In the final minutes, the Mustangs' discipline was tested when Jeremie Gould was assessed a delay-of-game penalty for flipping the puck over the glass in the defensive zone, but the defense tightened and successfully weathered the late pressure to preserve the 7-6 victory.
